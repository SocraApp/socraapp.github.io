// Questions, naturally

Good place to begin.

Still curious? That is rather the point.

Why not just use ChatGPT?

General-purpose AI gives useful answers quickly. Socra asks adaptive questions and helps you construct the answer yourself.

Does Socra ever provide answers?

Yes—when it is the right teaching move. It first looks for the smallest intervention that keeps your reasoning active.

Which AI models are supported?

Socra supports a selection of leading AI models. Availability can vary by plan.

Is Socra free?

Socra offers a free way to begin, with more usage and advanced capabilities on paid plans.

Can teachers use Socra?

Yes. Teachers can design question-led activities and help students practise independent reasoning.

Can Socra help with programming and exam preparation?

Yes. Trace control flow, debug assumptions, expose gaps, practise recall through explanation, and transfer ideas to unfamiliar problems.
